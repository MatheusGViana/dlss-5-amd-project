# v2.25 withdrawn

User reports broken neural effects and incorrect color in Spider-Man Remastered and Cyberpunk after enabling encoding modes. Local installed dxgi.dll hashes matched v2.25; both reverted to the v2.24 release with logs and INI backed up. AmdEncoding removed from installed INIs; other preferences preserved.

Do not distribute v2.25. Synthetic transfer round-trip and finite GPU output did not establish compatibility with neural exposure, pre-exposure, tonemap selection and the game SR color contract. Exact cause remains unconfirmed. Future encoding experiments must validate these together against real frames from both games and include a neural-disabled identity comparison. PCSX2 remains unsupported.
