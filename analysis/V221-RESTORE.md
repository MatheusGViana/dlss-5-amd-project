# v2.21 restore

The final distributable is the existing verified OptiScaler-AMD-PreSR-Multipass-v2.21.zip and its extracted directory. Its 29 manifest hashes were verified against the ZIP. Later experimental v2.22-v2.25 source edits were not rebuilt into this artifact. The evolving source tree was snapshotted separately before the next independent implementation.

The v2.25 encoding experiment is withdrawn after reports of broken neural effects and incorrect colors in Spider-Man and Cyberpunk. PCSX2 experimental presentation support is also not part of v2.21.
